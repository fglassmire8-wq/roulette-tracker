    How streaks are judged

    A streak is when the same kind of result comes up 5 or more spins in a row. The app checks:

    • Colour – red or black
    • Odd / even
    • Low (1–18) / high (19–36)
    • Dozen – 1st (1–12), 2nd (13–24), 3rd (25–36)
    • Column – the three columns of the table layout

    0 and 00 are green. They are not red/black, odd/even, low/high, or in any dozen or column, so they break those streaks.

    When a logged number makes a streak reach the set length, the app shows a banner, vibrates, and sends a notification if allowed. This alert happens once per streak. While the streak continues, a small box at the top shows it, e.g. “Red streak active · 6 in a row”, and the count updates with no further alert. The box disappears when the streak ends; undo recalculates it.

    Repeats of the same exact number are not streaks. They are counted in the History screen under “Most frequent so far”.

    Facts about this app: each spin is independent. The app only records and counts results that have already happened. It does not predict results or suggest bets.

    Scan a numberBeta

    Point the rear camera at a digital results display or screen showing one number, then tap Capture. Reads numbers only — it does not track the ball on a wheel. Camera use may not be allowed at casino tables; check the venue rules.

    Screen read Beta

    Reads digits from the picture of a tab or window you share — it does not read page data or connect to the site. Nothing is logged until you confirm.
